FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. The calculations run directly in your browser.

Do you store my measurements?

This frontend version does not send the calculator inputs to a server.

Is the concrete calculator a cement quantity calculator?

No. It calculates concrete volume. Cement, sand and aggregate depend on the specified mix design.
